### Changed

- **Claims replace the single session.** Until now awake held exactly one session, and anything that engaged replaced whatever was running: an agent's process watch could silently displace your indefinite session, and yours could displace the watch keeping an overnight job alive. Intent is now a set of claims that coexist: yours, an agent's, a build's timer, each its own entry. The Mac stays awake while any claim lives, and sleep restores when the last one ends, so the "Sleep restored" notification is a true sentence every time it appears.
- **The menu bar says who and why.** One roster line per owner: `you · indefinite`, `claude · while 2 processes run`, `release build · 42m left`. Process watches name themselves after the process; `--label` names anything else; pids stay in tooltips and the CLI. No more decoding a process table in your menu bar.
- **Right-click and ⌃⌥⌘A are the kill switch first.** If anything is holding the Mac awake, the toggle ends all of it; if nothing is, it starts your claim at the last duration you picked in the menu. The one item the gesture would hit wears the ⌃⌥⌘A badge, so the toggle's meaning is visible instead of folklore, and a checkmark means exactly one thing: your claim, running now, at that duration.
- **Only you teach the toggle its duration.** Picking a duration in the menu sets what right-click starts next time; a script or agent engaging from the shell no longer rewrites it.
- **"Keep display on" applies to your claims only.** An agent's background claim never lights the screen, which also stops closed-lid agent work from burning the battery to the floor for nothing.
- **Notifications only when they matter.** Sleep actually restored, a safety net firing, or your own claim lapsing while others still hold the Mac awake. An agent's claim quietly ending under other claims stays out of your face.

### Added

- **`awake off WHO`** ends just the claims matching an owner name or pid; bare `asleep` / `awake off` remains the end-everything gesture.
- **`awake --label NAME`** names a claim so the menu and notifications can say who wanted the Mac awake.
- **Engage replies explain themselves**: re-engaging as the same owner reports what it replaced, and engaging under a claim that already covers you says so, while still recording your claim so it takes over if the covering one ends first.
