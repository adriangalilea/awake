### Added

- **`awake display on|off` sets the standing "keep the screen on" preference from the shell**, which until now existed only as a checkbox in the menu. It applies to every way a session starts (menu, right-click, hotkey, CLI) and takes effect immediately: flip it during a running session and the display assertion is added or dropped there and then, no re-engaging. `awake display` on its own reports the current setting.

### Changed

- **Worth knowing if you expected Amphetamine's behaviour**: keeping the Mac awake and keeping the screen lit are separate here, and only the first is on by default. A dark screen during a session is normal, and it is also what arms the lock screen a few minutes later. Turn the preference on and both stop happening. `awake --display` remains the one-shot form for a single session.
