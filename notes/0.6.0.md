A closed Mac in a bag no longer cooks.

### Added

- **Critical heat ends lid-closed survival.** When macOS reports critical thermal pressure, awake ends every claim that keeps the Mac running with the lid shut, yours included, and closing the lid sleeps it again. Until it cools, new lid claims and lid grants are refused with the reason, so nothing re-arms on its own; claims that only hold the Mac awake with the lid open keep running. This exists because it happened: an indefinite claim, a Mac in a backpack, three hours at critical heat before macOS forced an emergency sleep with the chip at 95 °C and the battery still at 37 %, so the battery floor never fired.
- **You hear about it with the lid shut.** The end goes through the same channels as the battery floor: a banner on screen, and your `awake notify` hook if you set one, since a banner behind a closed lid informs nobody. The menu and `awake status` say "critical heat" while it lasts.
- **For scripts**: a claim ended this way reports `thermal` to its `--on-end` hook, and `status --json` gains `thermalCritical`.
