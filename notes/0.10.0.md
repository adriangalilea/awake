Simpler to install and to remove, and updates keep what you asked for.

### Changed

- **Installing and uninstalling awake are simpler.** awake keeps your Mac awake through a small background helper that macOS asks you to allow once. Open awake from the dmg or a download and it asks whether to install for everyone on the Mac or just for you, moves itself there and starts that helper. To uninstall, drag awake to the Trash: your Mac goes back to sleeping normally, and awake's settings, logs and commands go to the Trash too.

### Fixed

- **Updating awake keeps your sessions.** If you or a tool asked awake to keep the Mac awake, the new version picks that up where the old one left off, instead of the old version's helper carrying on after the update.
