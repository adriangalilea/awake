The menu bar icon now finishes its own setup.

### Fixed

- First-run dead icon: on a fresh machine the one-time privileged step (the scoped sudoers rule that lets awake flip lid-closed sleep) only existed as a terminal command, and without it every keep-awake gesture failed into a notification macOS had no permission to show.
- The menu now leads with "finish setup" until the grant works - one click, the system's own admin sheet, visudo-validated before anything lands.
- A keep-awake gesture that hits the missing grant raises the sheet itself and then completes what you asked for; cancelling stays quiet.
- A grant made in the terminal under a running daemon is picked up live.
