The menu bar icon now finishes its own setup.

On a fresh machine the one-time privileged step (the scoped sudoers rule
that lets awake flip lid-closed sleep) only existed as a terminal command,
and without it the icon looked dead: keep-awake gestures failed into a
notification macOS had no permission to show. Now the menu leads with
"finish setup" until the grant works, any keep-awake gesture raises the
native admin sheet itself and then completes what you asked for, and a
cancel stays quiet. `awake grant` still works and a grant made in the
terminal is picked up live.
