awake looks after itself.

### Changed

- **awake registers itself with macOS.** It is a regular background item now, listed in System Settings › General › Login Items, and it starts the first time you open the app or run any `awake` command. Installing with Homebrew no longer runs anything behind your back, and no longer prints deprecation warnings on every `brew` command.
- **Upgrades take effect on their own.** After `brew upgrade`, awake notices the new version within a minute and restarts into it, keeping every claim.
- **Deleting awake cleans up after itself.** Remove the app, with Homebrew or by dragging it to the Trash, and awake restores normal sleep and stops itself within two minutes, instead of leaving a process behind that macOS keeps trying to start.

### Fixed

- **Installing with Homebrew works again.** The 0.7.1 cask failed to install; this one installs cleanly.
